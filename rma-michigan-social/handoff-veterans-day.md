## Flags for the client / account team

1. **Flag colours (B).** The photo shows a real US flag, so red appears in B; A and C stay in the
   navy/cream palette.
2. **Photography (B).** The photo is AI-generated (a hand and the flag only, no face). Swap for
   licensed stock or client photography if preferred.
3. **Flag depiction (A).** A is a stylised, tone-on-tone flag (stars and stripes in brand navy and
   cream), not the official colours; confirm the client is comfortable with a brand-coloured flag.
4. **No added copy.** No date or eyebrow was added; the line already says "This Veterans Day".
