### Ask a Physician — motion infographic (3 versions)

The first round used four static beats with crossfades, which read as a slideshow. It was replaced
with three continuous motion infographics. Each is a single 1080×1350 frame on one 16 s timeline:
the stage persists, lines and rings draw on, elements move between states, captions rise in word by
word (Figma's native text stagger), and the logo stays fixed at the bottom. No slide cuts.

| Version | Concept |
| --- | --- |
| V1 — Two rings (navy) | A "you" ring draws on; a dashed partner ring with a "?" rotates in and turns solid ("often, yes"); a care-team badge links to both; Listening / Taking notes / Support chips branch from the partner; information dots stream in and the two rings move together, echoing the logo. |
| V2 — Note card (cream) | A note card rises; a question mark draws on; "Check with your care team" is written and ticked; Listening / Taking notes / Support slide in with underlines; lines of information fill the page and a second card slides in beside it. |
| V3 — Annotated photo | Slow push-in on the waiting-room photo; rings draw around the patient and the partner (dashed with "?" until "yes"); a care-team link, chips pinned to the partner, information dots, and a heart connector between them. |

**Selected: V2 Note card (cream)**, revised per feedback: the "Ask a Physician" eyebrow is removed;
the opening beat now uses V1's double circles (solid "you" ring with a person, dashed partner ring
that rotates in, line-drawn sans-serif "?"); in the last beat the card pile scales down and
re-centers, and the corner double-circle icon is removed. Frames:
`previews/nov15-28-ask-a-physician-note-card-selected-frames.png`.

The three MP4s (30 fps) were shared directly; sampled frames are in
`previews/nov15-28-ask-a-physician-motion-infographic-frames.png`. The chip labels (Listening,
Taking notes, Support) and "Check with your care team" are short labels lifted from the caption copy.

### Questions You Can Bring — round 2 (D, E, F)

Feedback on A–C was "I need something different… more creative but on-brand", so three new
directions sit directly under C in the Nov 22–28 section. All copy is the calendar's, verbatim.

| Version | Idea | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D — Conversation thread | The questions are messages the patient sends | Cream wash. Each slide shows the previous question faded above, the current one as a navy bubble, and a "typing" bubble for the next. The last slide is the care team's reply, in a white bubble with the two-ring mark as its avatar. |
| E — Follow the thread (panorama) | One line runs through all six slides | Navy. On the cover the line draws a question mark, then loops across the swipe, passing numbered stops 01–04 at each question, and ends in the logo's two rings behind the closing line. Questions alternate top/bottom so the line never crosses text. |
| F — Two circles | The logo's two rings become the layout | Cream wash. A photo circle overlaps an outline circle, alternating sides each slide; the question sits centred beneath. Photos: notebook (cover), results on a tablet, garden path, planner, water and berries, a quiet table by the window. |

Version E is built from one 6480×1350 component, "Version E panorama master", placed to the
right of the section. Each slide is an instance of it shifted by one slide width, so editing the
line or stops in the master updates all six slides and keeps the seams aligned.

## Flags for the client / account team

1. **Support-person policy.** Confirm RMA's current visitor/support-person policy before the
   Ask a Physician video is published, including whether it varies by appointment type (from the brief).
2. **Health plan post.** Confirm the CTA and the benefits-administrator guidance are accurate for
   RMA patients (from the brief).
3. **Men's Day eyebrow.** A small "International Men's Day • November 19" line was added so the
   graphic reads as an observance post; it is not in the calendar's graphic text and can be removed.
4. **Thanksgiving tone.** Kept to the calendar line only, with no "Happy Thanksgiving" or feast
   imagery, per the sensitivity note.
5. **Photography.** All photos are AI-generated placeholders (Figma image generation,
   896×1200, slightly upscaled). The cervical-day photo shows a clinician partly in profile; it is a
   generic scene and is not presented as RMA staff. Swap for licensed stock or client photography if
   preferred. (The selected Questions carousel, version D, uses no photos.) Round 2 adds three
   more AI photos: the hand with the ribbon (Cervical F), the linen flat lay (Thanksgiving E) and
   the hand with the leaf (Thanksgiving F). They show hands and objects only, no faces.
6. **Trend slots.** Both are empty templates. The 11/15–21 slot is not explicitly marked "ignore", but
   the calendar says to leave it open until the week of.
7. **Next step.** Once a direction is picked per post, the chosen frames can move into the
   matching sections on the client's "Nov-Dec RMA Content" page.
8. **Ribbon colour.** The cervical cancer awareness ribbon is teal and white. Cervical F shows it
   in those colours because it is a real object in a photo; D and E draw it in brand navy and
   white. Confirm the client is happy with teal appearing in F.
9. **Cervical D labels.** "Preventive care" and "Reproductive health" inside the two circles are
   lifted from the calendar line, not separate copy. They can be removed if the client wants the
   circles unlabelled.
10. **Thanksgiving E logo.** The logo sits small on the note card, like a sign-off, instead of at
    the bottom of the frame, because the photo has the tea and eucalyptus there.
