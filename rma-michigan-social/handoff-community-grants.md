## The three options

| Option | Look | How the carousel works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — Resource cards | Navy with the soft glow, white cards | The cover shows three cards fanned out, one per resource. Each resource slide deals one card forward (index dot, organisation name, headline, body, line icon) with the remaining cards peeking underneath, so the stack shrinks as you swipe. The closing slide has the two rings, the line and the link in an outlined box. |
| B — Folder tabs | Cream wash, white folder page | A folder with three numbered tabs. The cover page lists the three resources like a table of contents; each resource slide opens its tab (01, 02, 03) with the details on the page. The closing slide shows the folder with the line and the link. |
| C — Photo-led | AI photo cover and close, navy resource slides | The cover is a photo of two people's hands going through application paperwork together, with the title over a navy fade. Each resource slide is navy with a large line icon, the organisation name, headline and body. The closing slide reuses the photo under a deep navy wash. |

Line icons, drawn for this post and used in all three options: branching paths (Baby Quest
Foundation, "more than one path to parenthood"), two people (Jewish Fertility Foundation,
"community support") and a document with a check (Christen Goff RMA IVF Grant).

## Flags for the client / account team

1. **Slide count.** The brief says "Carousel post — 4 slides" but the graphic text lists five
   slides (cover, three resources, closing). All three options follow the graphic text and have
   five slides.
2. **Organisation names on slides 2–4.** The names come from the brief's "(Highlighting …)"
   notes and are used as small labels so each slide makes sense on its own. If the client wants
   the partner logos instead, they need to supply them and confirm we have permission to use them.
3. **Link.** The URL is shown in full as written: "https://www.rmami.com/grants.html". It could be
   shortened to "rmami.com/grants" for legibility. Links in Instagram posts are not clickable.
4. **Facts to confirm before publishing.** The $20,000 amount, Michigan and Ohio eligibility, the
   Brilora Fertility Foundation as the grant's sponsor, and each programme's offering should be
   checked against the current programme details.
5. **Photography (option C).** The photo is an AI-generated placeholder showing hands and objects
   only, no faces and no money. Swap for licensed stock or client photography if preferred.
