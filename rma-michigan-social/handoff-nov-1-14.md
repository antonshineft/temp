# RMA of Michigan — November 1–14 social graphics (handoff)

Figma (Sandbox page): https://www.figma.com/design/4EVDWThiP9h3nuSMMQoArn/RMA-of-Michigan--Anton-?node-id=35-2

All posts are 1080×1350 (Instagram portrait), matching the Sept–Oct file. Each week sits in its own
section on the **Sandbox** page, laid out the same way as the existing "KH" sections, with a
"Notes for review" block next to row 1.

## Brand references used

- Type: Lora (Bold / SemiBold / Regular) for all display and body copy; DM Sans Medium for small
  date lines. Both already in use in the Sept–Oct posts.
- Color: navy `#003887` / `#0e3459`, white, cream wash `#f3e6d5`, and the existing photo overlay
  gradient (`#141414` 0% → `#09264a` 93%) and navy glow gradient copied from existing posts.
- Logo: the ring logo was cloned from the existing posts into two local components on the Sandbox
  page (`RMA Logo / White`, `RMA Logo / Navy`) and placed as instances.

## Round 2 — image variations and enhancements

- **Image variations.** Two extra cover photos (v2, v3) for World Fertility Day, Open Enrollment,
  NP Week, Fertility Testing, and Transgender Awareness Week. Row-1 variations sit beside the
  originals; carousel-cover variations sit in a labelled row at the bottom of each section.
- **Open Enrollment carousel.** Slide counter (01 / 05 …) at the top of every slide, a
  question-mark icon in place of the "Question N" eyebrow, and a soft top scrim on the photo cover.
- **Ask a Physician storyboard.** Line icons on frames 1–3 (speech bubble, clock, calendar +
  clipboard + target). On the last frame the closing line sits inside a large background speech
  bubble with a brighter glow. The logo stays fixed at the bottom of every frame (no fade, same
  position), including through the transitions in the single-file video. Each frame carries a 3 s keyframe animation (glow bloom, icon pop,
  staggered copy, logo fade) and the four frames are linked as a timed prototype flow. A separate
  master frame (to the right of the slides) holds all four on one 13.4 s timeline with crossfade +
  push transitions; export it from Figma (Present → Export video) for a single MP4. Sampled frames
  are in `previews/storyboard-full-video-frames.png`.
- **Fertility Testing carousel.** A continuous numbered step rail runs across the top of all five
  slides; slide 3 lists the four test areas with icons (cycle, hourglass, anatomy, sperm) and slides
  2, 4, 5 carry a matching icon (history, checklist, magnifier).
- **Icons** are drawn in one style (3 px line, rounded caps) and live as editable vectors in each
  frame.

## Trend slot — red-card carousel

Built from the October Trend Slot copy (RMA section only) and placed in the 11/8–14 trend-slot row,
replacing that week's empty template. Brand navy background with the glow gradient (same as the
other carousels), one misconception per slide set on a tilted red card in oversized Lora Bold,
clarification beneath in white, slide counter, small red-card glyph, and the RMA logo kept small.
The cover uses an AI-generated photo of a hand holding up a red card on a plain navy background,
blended into the frame. Copy is verbatim from the doc. Preview: `previews/trend-red-card-carousel.png`.

## Flags for the client / account team

1. **NP Week needs a real photo.** The brief asks for a team or candid clinic photo. The current
   image is an AI-generated, faceless clinic placeholder so no generated person is presented as
   RMA staff. Please request a high-res photo of RMA's nurse practitioners with room for text in
   the lower third.
2. **Clinical accuracy sign-off.** Fertility Testing slide 3 (ovulation, ovarian reserve,
   reproductive anatomy, sperm) and the wording on how testing is selected need RMA review before
   publishing. Transgender Awareness Week copy on fertility preservation also needs confirmation of
   RMA-specific services.
3. **Open Enrollment blog link.** Confirm "Fertility Benefits and Open Enrollment: What to Ask Your
   Employer" is live before the post goes out (caption says "link in bio").
4. **Ask a Physician is briefed as video.** The four frames are the storyboard beats and double as
   a static fallback; the motion edit (or a physician on camera) still has to be produced. A 9:16
   version can be derived from these frames.
5. **Photography.** The five cover photos (World Fertility Day, Open Enrollment, NP Week, Fertility
   Testing, Transgender Awareness Week) are AI-generated placeholders made with Figma's image
   generation at 896×1200, so they are slightly upscaled to 1080×1350. Swap for licensed stock or
   client photography if preferred; the layers are named "Photo (AI-generated placeholder)".
6. **Open Enrollment v2 photo** shows the word "SUMMARY" on the booklet (AI artifact). Fine as a
   variation, but retouch or swap if it is the pick.
7. **Red-card trend post.** Topics need clinical review before publishing (AMH, IVF pathways,
   first-test wording). If it belongs in the 11/1–7 slot instead, the row can be moved.
8. **Brand assets.** RMA has no brand guidelines document. A vector logo file, any approved
   photography library, and confirmation of Lora as the brand typeface would make future batches
   faster and more consistent.
