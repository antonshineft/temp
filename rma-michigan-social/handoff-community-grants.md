## Flags for the client / account team

1. **Slide count.** The brief says "Carousel post — 4 slides" but the graphic text lists five
   slides (cover, three resources, closing). Every option has five slides.
2. **Organisation names on slides 2–4** (and on the covers that list them). The names come from the
   brief's "(Highlighting …)" notes. If the client wants the partner logos instead, they need to
   supply them and confirm we have permission to use them.
3. **Link.** The URL is shown in full as written: "https://www.rmami.com/grants.html". It could be
   shortened to "rmami.com/grants" for legibility. Links in Instagram posts are not clickable.
4. **Facts to confirm before publishing.** The $20,000 amount, Michigan and Ohio eligibility, the
   Brilora Fertility Foundation as the grant's sponsor, and each programme's offering should be
   checked against the current programme details.
5. **Photography.** Covers 1 and 3 use AI-generated placeholder photos (a desk flat lay; hands
   opening a folder). Hands and objects only, no faces and no money. Swap for licensed stock or
   client photography if preferred.
6. **Styling only.** The numerals in D and the marker underlines in E are visual treatments; no
   words were added or changed.
