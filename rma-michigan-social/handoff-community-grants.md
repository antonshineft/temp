## Flags for the client / account team

1. **Slide count.** The brief says "Carousel post — 4 slides" but the graphic text lists five
   slides (cover, three resources, closing). Every option has five slides.
2. **Partner logos need approval and official files.** The logos were taken from the organisations'
   websites and are placeholders until the client confirms permission to use them and supplies
   official files. Baby Quest's site only carries a reversed (white) logo, so a one-colour dark
   version (light slides) and an all-white version (navy slides) were made from the same artwork;
   JFF and Brilora white versions for option F were made the same way. The JFF logo comes from a
   512 px image, so a vector file would print sharper.
3. **Christen Goff RMA IVF Grant logo.** No logo exists for the grant itself; its slide uses the
   Brilora Fertility Foundation logo because the brief says the grant is offered through Brilora.
   Confirm this is the right attribution.
4. **Link.** The URL is shown in full as written: "https://www.rmami.com/grants.html". It could be
   shortened to "rmami.com/grants" for legibility. Links in Instagram posts are not clickable.
5. **Facts to confirm before publishing.** The $20,000 amount, Michigan and Ohio eligibility, the
   Brilora Fertility Foundation as the grant's sponsor, and each programme's offering should be
   checked against the current programme details.
6. **Photography.** Covers 1 and 3 use AI-generated placeholder photos (a desk flat lay; hands
   opening a folder). Hands and objects only, no faces and no money. Swap for licensed stock or
   client photography if preferred.
7. **Styling only.** The numerals in D and the marker underlines in E are visual treatments; no
   words were added or changed.
